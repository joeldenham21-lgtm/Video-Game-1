# THIN AIR — Audio notes

Everything audible in the game except the score is generated by `tools/audio/` (Python 3.12 + numpy/scipy,
sox/ffmpeg, piper TTS). Nothing is sampled from outside sources. Rebuild: see `tools/README.md`.

## For other workstreams (integration)

- **Just emit Events.** `Audio` already sonifies: `footstep` (surface; `step_snow_cold` below −10 °C; crampons
  layered on snow/ice when `player.has_gear(&"crampons")`), `player_landed`, `player_damaged` (pain types ≥ 2 hp),
  `player_died` (+ death stinger, music silence), `item_picked_up/dropped/crafted/consumed` (eat vs drink from
  ItemDB category), `equipment_changed`, `blueprint_unlocked` (+ stinger), `scan_completed`,
  `ui_screen_opened/closed` (`inventory`/`crafting` zip sounds; `pause` pauses voice), `objective_added` (notify),
  `objective_completed`/`poi_discovered` (stingers), `log_found`, `game_started`, `player_respawned`.
  Calling the same ids yourself is harmless: each id (or group — all footsteps share one) has a cooldown and
  plays once per moment/place.
- **Loops:** `play_loop(id, node)` returns an owned `AudioStreamPlayer3D` (fades in; free it to stop).
  Generator: `Audio.start_loop_with_intro(&"generator_start", &"generator_loop", shed_node)` cranks, catches and
  crossfades into the loop. The helicopter loop is dry mono with Doppler enabled — attach it to the moving node.
- **Voice:** `Audio.play_voice(id)` → duration; subtitles arrive per sentence through `Events.subtitle(speaker,
  text, duration)` (`speaker` = display name; `Events.subtitle("", "", 0)` after `stop_voice()` means clear).
  `Audio.play_voice_3d(id, node)` plays positionally (the wreck's radio). Sam never speaks: on the radio he answers
  Mara with mic clicks (subtitled "[keys the radio]"). The whole prologue is one scene: `play_voice(&"prologue")`
  (≈3 min, stereo; `voice.json["prologue"].events` gives engine_rough / engine_quit / brace / impact times).
- **Music:** `set_music_state` accepts the contract states. `explore` is automatic (refined to night / forest /
  alpine / blizzard from Climate + altitude + forest mask when cues exist). `danger` auto-releases after 45 s unless
  re-requested. Cue ids in `data/music.json` map to states by prefix (`forest_2` → forest, `stinger_discovery`
  → discovery stinger) or explicit `"state"`/`"stinger"` fields. Ambient states: a cue plays about once (≤ 3 min; loop cues fade out
  with their own `fade_out_s` on the last `phrase_starts_s` boundary before the end), then 1–4 min of silence;
  urgent states (danger, blizzard, summit, finale, menu) start immediately (cue `fade_in_s`) and continue — loop
  cues loop from `loop_offset_s`, through-composed ones repeat after an 8–15 s breath.
- **Environment:** `set_environment_reverb(&"cave"|&"interior"|&"station"|&"forest")`; `&"outdoor"` returns to
  automatic detection (Climate shelter ≥ 0.85 → interior, forest mask > 0.5 → forest). `station` switches the
  ambience to the station interior (hum if `Game.flags.station_power` or `generator_loop` is playing, silent and
  creaking otherwise). `set_muffled(0..1)` sweeps the Master low-pass 20.5 kHz → 320 Hz.
- **Flags read:** `station_power` (bool), `wolves_silent` (bool, suppresses distant phantom howls).
- **More hooks:** `Events.radio_message(id)` plays that voice line (a direct `play_voice` of the same line in the
  same moment does not restart it); `sleep_started`/`sleep_ended` muffle the mix and pause the score; death muffles
  until `player_respawned`. `Audio.stop_all()` silences everything at once (world teardown).
- **Mobile** (`Settings.is_mobile()`): 16 instead of 24 pooled 3D voices, at most 6 ambience beds decoding at once
  (the weakest are dropped), otherwise identical behaviour.

## Engine (src/autoload/audio.gd, src/audio/*)

- 24 pooled `AudioStreamPlayer3D` (inverse-distance, per-id `unit_size`/`max_distance`, distance darkening via
  the attenuation filter, Doppler only where `doppler: true`), 8 pooled 2D players, 4 UI players that run while
  paused. Voice stealing: per-id `max_voices`, then oldest low-priority (ambient) voice, then oldest. Sounds beyond
  `max_distance` are culled before playing.
- Bus routing is fixed at runtime to match the contract: `SFX` and `Ambience` send to `Reverb` whose reverb
  runs with `dry = 1` (wet per environment preset, interpolated over ~1 s).
- **Ambience manager** blends 17 stereo beds every 0.25 s: wind layers (calm/breeze/strong/gale partitioned
  over effective wind = `Climate.get_wind_at` × terrain exposure (ridge vs gully from `TerrainData.get_height`)
  × forest shelter), alpine howl (altitude > 2,150 m), spruce soughing (forest mask × wind), blizzard, forest
  day/night (daylight), water (creek/river/lake/waterfall distances from `TerrainData.layout` rivers/lakes/POIs,
  falling back to probing `get_water_level`), cave, station, cabin interior. Positional water emitters sit at the
  nearest point of the nearest river/lake/waterfall so water has direction. Beds duck −3 dB under voice.
  A sparse **spatial event scheduler** (Subnautica-style phantom life) places one-shots around the listener by
  context: chickadees/nuthatch/kinglet/crossbill/junco/jay by day in forest, woodpecker drumming, ravens,
  golden eagle above 1,700 m, ptarmigan and Clark's nutcracker in their altitude bands, owls at night, distant
  wolf howls at night, rockfall/serac falls/avalanches at altitude, lake/glacier ice cracks when cold, trunk
  creaks in wind, station frame creaks, rare thunder in blizzards.

## Loudness standards

Measured with BS.1770 (own implementation, verified against `ffmpeg ebur128`): voice −18 LUFS integrated;
footsteps −25 LUFS (loudest 100 ms); tools −15; big events −13; creatures −14; UI −27; breathing −27; beds at
natural relative levels (calm wind −36 … waterfall/blizzard −17 LUFS integrated). True peak ≤ −1 dBTP everywhere;
transient sounds are never limited more than 3 dB (they end up below target rather than squashed). All OGG
Vorbis 44.1 kHz; positional SFX mono, beds and the prologue stereo.

## Synthesis models (tools/audio/lib, tools/audio/gen)

- **Footsteps**: heel strike + roll + toe-off, each exciting a surface model. Boot = body mass-spring thump
  (70–130 Hz) + rubber slap. Snow = Poisson cloud of bond fractures (band-limited clicks, log-uniform 0.7–4.5 kHz)
  whose rate follows the compaction curve + low "whump"; cold snow adds quasi-periodic stick-slip squeak chains
  (650–1100 Hz). Scree/gravel = modal stone pings (inharmonic ratios, size-scaled) + grit scrape + bouncing pebble
  (decreasing intervals). Wood = plank beam modes + cavity + stick-slip creak. Metal = plate modes damped by the
  sole. Ice = hard tock + short glassy plate ring. Water = Minnaert bubbles + slosh + drips. Crampons = 12 point
  strikes with small steel modes.
- **Water**: Minnaert bubbles (f = 3.26/r, damping 0.043f + 0.0014f^1.5, rising pitch — van den Doel 2005) in
  populations with power-law radii; creeks add gurgle bursts and flow rush; splashes add slap, cavity collapse,
  spray patter.
- **Wood/metal/stone**: modal synthesis (free-free beam ratios for logs/planks with frequency-dependent loss;
  plate ratios for steel; long-ringing axe heads on stone, damped when embedded in wood). Creaks = stick-slip
  friction pulse trains through the structure's modes. Tree fall = rigid-body fall integration (θ'' = 3g/2L sinθ)
  driving crown whoosh level/brightness, branch snaps concentrated late; impact = ground thud + bounce + crown crash.
- **Ice crack**: flexural waves in floating ice are dispersive (group delay ∝ 1/√ω), built in the frequency
  domain → the descending "pew" of singing ice, plus shore echoes and a low boom.
- **Thunder**: a random-walk lightning channel; every 12 m segment radiates an N-wave arriving after its path
  distance/c, atmospheric absorption by distance, valley echoes (Few 1969).
- **Fire**: turbulent low roar with flicker, gas hiss, clustered resin crackles, pops with woody body, sap sizzle,
  ember tinkles. Torch: more flutter; flare: fierce sputtering hiss.
- **Voices (animals, player)**: source–filter. Tonal calls use an additive harmonic source (tilt, jitter,
  shimmer, subharmonics) read through time-varying formants; rough calls (growls, croaks, bear) use jittered
  glottal pulse trains with period-doubling through formant filter banks; aspiration noise is shaped by the same
  envelopes. Wolf howl: slide-up onset, plateau undulations, register breaks, falling end, valley echoes, pack
  chorus. Birds are near-pure FM tones with weak harmonics on field-guide contours (chickadee fee-bee 4.0/3.4 kHz,
  chick-a-dee "dee" harmonic stacks, nuthatch nasal "yank", kinglet 7.6 kHz, crossbill, junco, Canada jay, owls).
- **Wind**: circularly periodic noise shaped per STFT frame by a gust process (log-normal speed): level ∝ v^2.4,
  spectral centre ∝ v^1.1, aeolian whistles at f ∝ v above thresholds, ear buffeting at high speed; stereo gust
  fronts sweep across the listener. All beds are exactly periodic (events and reverb tails are folded onto the loop).
- **Machines**: 3-cylinder Kubota diesel at 1,800 rpm (45 Hz firing, block modes, alternator 120 Hz hum, fan)
  built on an exact 2,940-sample cycle; helicopter = 2-blade main rotor slap at 10.77 Hz on an exact 4,096-sample
  period, tail rotor, turbine tones, gearbox whine; Otter R-1340 radial (9-cyl firing, 3-blade geared prop).

(YIN F0, pitch range, spectral centroid/tilt, rate — `voice/cast_voices.py`), then shortlisted candidates were
auditioned by spectrogram and noise floor (`voice/audition.py`); voices with hum, noise or artefacts were rejected.

The script (79 lines, ≈27 minutes of speech plus the prologue) was written against DESIGN.md §2: understated,
concrete (Otter C-FKTL, R-1340 carb/induction icing, AWOS format, channel 6, Kubota 6 kW on gelling summer diesel,
mass balance −1.4 m w.e., icefall velocities, a water-split transceiver), with radio procedure (callsigns, "say souls
on board and fuel", mayday format) and no melodrama.

Delivery: per-speaker `length_scale`/`noise_scale`/`noise_w`, slower pacing on the heaviest lines; every sentence
is rendered separately and re-timed with punctuation-dependent pauses, soft synthesized inhales, and exact
per-sentence subtitle timing. Processing: **radio** = 280–3,500 Hz, small-speaker EQ, fast AGC, soft clipping,
FM capture-effect noise floor, squelch-open burst and "kssh" tail per transmission, PTT clicks; transmissions
split around Sam's mic clicks. **Weak signal** (beacon, broken replies) ≈ 12 dB SNR with multipath flutter,
picket-fencing micro-dropouts and scripted dropouts. **Dictaphone** = small room / ice-cave reverb or summit wind
on the mic, 180–6,500 Hz, AGC, wow (0.62 Hz) & flutter (7.3 Hz), tape hiss, capstan whine, record/stop key
clunks; the ice-cave logs use a short bright ice-cave room (Sabine RT ≈ 1.2 s with a snow floor) and meltwater drips,
Hale's summit log has wind buffeting the recorder mic. **Cockpit** = boom-mic intercom band + the R-1340 under it; **in person** = station module room.

## QA

`tools/audio/qa_audio.py` decodes every shipped OGG: ebur128 loudness and true peak, channel layout, duration,
loop seams (decoded end→start discontinuity and level step). During development every generator was reviewed as
log-frequency spectrograms + waveform (`build_sfx.py --preview`, `contact_sheet.py`); issues found and fixed that
way included over-bright snow grains, wind beds collapsing to silence between gusts, truncated resonance tails,
modal onset clicks, ice-crack dispersion 3× too slow, and unintelligible weak-radio SNR.

QA status (last run): 482 files, all ≤ −1.0 dBTP after encoding (the writers decode and re-check every OGG),
all loops continuous at the seam (end→start jump ≤ 1.2× a typical 90th-percentile sample step, i.e. inaudible), mono/stereo layout as specified. Wind beds were retuned
so strong/gale winds swell by 10–14 dB (p10–p90) without ever dropping to a calm-day hush between gusts.

Script cost (tests/test_audio.gd, headless, contended 4-core container): the whole audio frame — Audio, ambience
blend + context + water search + event scheduler, music director — costs ≈ 0.33 ms in the worst case where every
timer fires in the same frame; a typical frame is a fraction of that.

## Known gaps

- Music is composed by the music stream; until `data/music.json` exists the director is silent (by design).
- Phantom ambient animals are not linked to the fauna simulation (real animals should play their own ids).
- Voices are neural TTS: natural for radio/dictaphone, but emotional range is limited compared to actors.
